Assistant's Answer: Our security policy is designed to ensure a respectful and safe workplace environment for all employees, and it includes several key points:

3. Confidential business and personal information of customers and employees must be safeguarded.
4. Employees must use company systems and devices responsibly, and unauthorized access is strictly prohibited.
5. Any potential conflict of interest must be reported, and accepting inappropriate gifts or benefits is not allowed.

7. All employees must report any suspected misconduct and security incidents immediately.

3. **Confidentiality**: It's crucial to safeguard personal and confidential information of both employees and customers. This is vital for maintaining trust and compliance with privacy regulations.

5. **Conflict of Interest**: Employees are required to report any potential conflicts of interest and must not accept inappropriate gifts or benefits that could influence their professional judgment.

6. **Zero Tolerance for Corruption**: Bribery, corruption, and fraudulent activities are categorically banned. This ensures a fair and ethical workplace.

7. **Incident Reporting**: Employees must report any suspected misconduct or security incidents immediately. This quick action helps mitigate risks and reinforces accountability.

8. **Disciplinary Measures**: Violations of the security policy may lead to disciplinary action, with the severity of the consequences based on the nature of the violation. This underscores the importance of adherence to the policy.

9. **Access Control**: Implementing access control measures ensures that only authorized personnel can access sensitive information and areas, significantly enhancing security.

11. **Emergency Procedures**: Establish clear emergency procedures, including evacuation plans and communication strategies, to ensure preparedness during unforeseen events (Garda).
