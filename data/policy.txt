1. All employees must maintain professional and respectful behavior at work.
2. Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and visitors with respect.
3. Discrimination based on gender, age, religion, race, or background is prohibited.
4. Harassment, bullying, intimidation, and threatening behavior are not permitted.
5. Employees must communicate professionally in person and through digital channels.
6. Employees must follow all company policies, procedures, and applicable laws.
7. Company property must be used responsibly and protected from damage or misuse.
8. Employees must keep company information confidential.
9. Confidential business information must not be shared with unauthorized persons.
10. Employees must protect customer and employee personal information.
11. Employees must use company systems and devices responsibly.
12. Unauthorized access to company systems is strictly prohibited.
13. Employees must maintain accurate and honest work records.
14. Falsifying documents, reports, or business information is prohibited.
15. Employees must avoid conflicts of interest that may affect business decisions.
16. Any potential conflict of interest should be reported to HR or management.
17. Employees must not accept inappropriate gifts or benefits from business partners.
18. Bribery, corruption, and fraudulent activities are strictly prohibited.
19. Employees must follow workplace health and safety requirements.
20. Employees must report unsafe workplace conditions promptly.
21. Possession or use of illegal drugs at the workplace is prohibited.
22. Employees must follow the company's rules regarding alcohol consumption.
23. Violence or threats of violence in the workplace are prohibited.
24. Employees must maintain appropriate workplace attire and appearance.
25. Employees must use company communication channels appropriately.
26. Offensive, abusive, or inappropriate content must not be shared at work.
27. Employees must respect company intellectual property.
28. Copyrighted or licensed materials must be used according to applicable rules.
29. Employees must not misuse company resources for personal gain.
30. Employees should maintain punctuality and follow assigned working hours.
31. Employees must inform their manager when they are unable to attend work.
32. Remote employees must follow company rules for working from home.
33. Employees must maintain professional behavior during business travel and events.
34. Company expenses must be reported accurately and honestly.
35. Employees must not submit false or unauthorized expense claims.
36. Employees should cooperate with internal investigations when required.
37. Employees must report suspected misconduct through appropriate channels.
38. Employees who report misconduct in good faith should not face retaliation.
39. Managers are responsible for promoting an ethical workplace environment.
40. Employees must respect the privacy and personal boundaries of colleagues.
41. Social media activity must not disclose confidential company information.
42. Employees should not represent personal opinions as official company statements.
43. Any suspected security incident must be reported immediately.
44. Employees must complete mandatory compliance and training programs.
45. Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action.
46. Disciplinary action will depend on the nature and seriousness of the violation.
47. Serious violations may result in termination of employment.
48. Employees may contact HR for clarification about this policy.
49. The company may update this Code of Conduct when necessary.
50. All employees are expected to read, understand, and follow this policy.
